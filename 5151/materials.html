02 · Pre-reading

Lecture notes

This is a flipped course: read the notes before the session. Weeks 1 and 2 were taught together in the combined first session on 9 September.

  • Week 1 — Introduction to Agentic AI & Research Methodology. pre-read Working definition of an agent, the autonomy spectrum, agent anatomy, and the three-pass method for reading a paper.
  • Week 2 — Reasoning & Planning. pre-read Chain-of-Thought, self-consistency, ReAct, Reflexion, Tree- and Graph-of-Thoughts, LATS, and where planning claims outrun their evidence.
  • Week 3 — Tools, MCP, Context & Evaluation. read before 16 Sep Function calling, the Model Context Protocol, context engineering, and how to evaluate an agent on more than final-answer accuracy.
03 · Seminars & project

Choosing your topics

  • Seminar topics. read before the form All 16 topics — 8 core for Seminar 1, 8 advanced for Seminar 2 — with the core readings, what every talk must explain, the presentation rota, and the grading rubric. Each seminar is 25% of your grade, and the critical-analysis section is where the marks are. The same content is on the Seminars page.
  • Project guidelines. read before the 1-pager The topic-quality gate (novel · non-trivial · meaningful · feasible in ~10 weeks), requirements R1–R9, the deliverable schedule D1–D4, and the grading criteria including the 0.8–1.1 individual factor. The same content is on the Project page.
Pick them together One group carries two seminar topics and one project all semester. Rank seminar topics that feed the system you intend to build, not the ones that merely sound interesting — otherwise you read twice as much and reuse none of it.
